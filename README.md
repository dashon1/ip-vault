# The IP Vault — Protect Everything You've Built

A complete trademark, copyright, and business structure protection system used by entrepreneurs to secure their brand and business assets before someone else does.

---

## What It Is

The IP Vault is a step-by-step intellectual property protection system that walks you through registering trademarks, filing copyrights, forming LLCs or corporations, and putting the right legal structures in place to protect your brand. It's built for entrepreneurs, creators, and small business owners who need real protection without paying attorney fees for every step.

The product combines guides, templates, checklists, case studies, and an interactive workbook into a single system you can execute yourself or hand to a professional to execute faster.
