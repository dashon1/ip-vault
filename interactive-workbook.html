STEP 4

Delaware C-Corp Setup (If Seeking VC)

Is C-Corp Right for You?
Choose C-Corp if:
  • Seeking venture capital or angel investment
  • Planning to IPO or be acquired
  • Have multiple co-founders with equity
  • Need to issue stock options to employees
  • Building a high-growth tech company
Yes, pursuing VC
Maybe in future
LLC is fine for now
Formation Checklist
  • Delaware name search - corp.delaware.gov
  • Registered agent selected - Delaware agent
  • Certificate of Incorporation filed - $89 filing fee
  • Received certified copy - Official document
  • First board meeting held - Via consent or video
  • Bylaws adopted - Corporate governance
  • Officers appointed - CEO, CFO, Secretary
  • Founder stock issued - Stock purchase agreements
  • IP assigned to company - Assignment agreements
  • 83(b) elections filed - Within 30 days!
