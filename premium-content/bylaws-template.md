# BYLAWS OF [CORPORATION NAME]

**A Delaware Corporation**

---

## ARTICLE I: OFFICES

### Section 1.1 Principal Office
The principal office of the Corporation shall be located in [CITY, STATE]. The Corporation may have other offices as the Board of Directors may designate.

### Section 1.2 Registered Office
The registered office of the Corporation in the State of Delaware shall be at [ADDRESS]. The Corporation may change its registered office from time to time as permitted by law.

---

## ARTICLE II: SHAREHOLDERS

### Section 2.1 Annual Meeting
The annual meeting of shareholders shall be held on [DATE] at [TIME] at the principal office or such other place as the Board may designate.

### Section 2.2 Special Meetings
Special meetings of shareholders may be called by the Board, the Chairman, or holders of at least 10% of outstanding stock.

### Section 2.3 Notice of Meetings
Written notice of each meeting shall be given not less than 10 nor more than 60 days before the meeting date to each shareholder of record.

### Section 2.4 Quorum
A majority of the outstanding shares entitled to vote constitutes a quorum. If a quorum is present, actions are approved by majority vote.

### Section 2.5 Voting Rights
Each share is entitled to one vote. Shareholders may vote in person or by written proxy.

---

## ARTICLE III: BOARD OF DIRECTORS

### Section 3.1 Number and Qualification
The Board shall consist of [NUMBER] directors. The number may be changed by amendment to these Bylaws.

### Section 3.2 Election and Term
Directors are elected at the annual meeting for a term of one year or until their successor is elected.

### Section 3.3 Quorum
A majority of the directors constitutes a quorum. Actions are approved by majority vote of those present.

### Section 3.4 Meetings
The Board shall meet at least quarterly. Meetings may be held in person, by phone, or video conference.

### Section 3.5 Compensation
Directors may receive reasonable compensation for their services as determined by the Board.

### Section 3.6 Removal
Any director may be removed with or without cause by the shareholders holding a majority of shares.

---

## ARTICLE IV: OFFICERS

### Section 4.1 Officers Defined
The officers of the Corporation shall include:
- **Chairman of the Board** (optional)
- **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**
- **President**
- **Vice President**
- **Secretary**
- **Chief Financial Officer (CFO)** or Treasurer

### Section 4.2 Election
Officers are elected annually by the Board at its first meeting following the annual shareholder meeting.

### Section 4.3 Duties

**Chairman of the Board:**
- Presides at shareholder and Board meetings
- Performs duties as assigned by the Board

**CEO:**
- General supervision of business operations
- Signs contracts and documents as authorized
- Reports to the Board

**Secretary:**
- Maintains corporate records and minutes
- Gives notice of meetings
- Attests to corporate documents

**CFO/Treasurer:**
- Maintains financial records
- Supervises corporate finances
- Provides financial reports to the Board

### Section 4.4 Removal
Any officer may be removed with or without cause by the Board.

### Section 4.5 Bonding
The Corporation may require officers to be bonded as the Board determines.

---

## ARTICLE V: COMMITTEES

### Section 5.1 Executive Committee
The Board may create an Executive Committee with authority to act on certain matters between Board meetings.

### Section 5.2 Audit Committee
If required, an Audit Committee shall oversee financial reporting and audits.

### Section 5.3 Compensation Committee
A Compensation Committee may be formed to determine executive compensation.

### Section 5.4 Other Committees
The Board may create other committees as needed with authority designated by the Board.

---

## ARTICLE VI: STOCK

### Section 6.1 Certificates
Stock shall be represented by certificates signed by the CEO or President and Secretary.

### Section 6.2 Transfer
Stock is transferable on the books of the Corporation subject to applicable laws and these Bylaws.

### Section 6.3 Loss of Certificates
The Corporation may issue new certificates upon proof of loss and such security as the Board determines.

### Section 6.4 Transfer Restrictions
Stock may be subject to transfer restrictions as set forth in stockholder agreements.

---

## ARTICLE VII: DIVIDENDS

### Section 7.1 Declaration
Dividends may be declared by the Board at any meeting subject to applicable law and the Certificate of Incorporation.

### Section 7.2 Payment
Dividends shall be paid to shareholders of record on the date determined by the Board.

---

## ARTICLE VIII: FISCAL YEAR

### Section 8.1 Fiscal Year
The fiscal year of the Corporation shall end on [DATE] each year, unless changed by the Board.

---

## ARTICLE IX: AMENDMENTS

### Section 9.1 Power to Amend
These Bylaws may be amended or repealed by:
- Shareholders holding a majority of shares, or
- The Board by majority vote

---

## ARTICLE X: INDEMNIFICATION

### Section 10.1 Indemnification
The Corporation shall indemnify its directors and officers to the fullest extent permitted by Delaware law.

### Section 10.2 Insurance
The Corporation may purchase directors and officers liability insurance.

---

## ARTICLE XI: CORPORATE Seal

### Section 11.1 Seal
The corporate seal shall be [DESCRIPTION OF SEAL]. The seal may be affixed to documents by the Secretary or designated officers.

---

## ARTICLE XII: EXECUTION OF INSTRUMENTS

### Section 12.1 Signing Authority
Contracts, checks, and other documents shall be signed by such officers as authorized by the Board.

### Section 12.2 Bank Accounts
The Board shall designate officers authorized to sign checks and withdrawals.

---

## CERTIFICATE OF ADOPTION

The undersigned, being the Secretary of [CORPORATION NAME], hereby certifies that these Bylaws were adopted by the Board of Directors on [DATE].
