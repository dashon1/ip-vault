## ARTICLE 2: PURPOSE

### 2.1 Purpose
The Company is formed for the purpose of engaging in any lawful business activity for which LLCs may be organized in the State of [STATE].

### 2.2 Powers
The Company shall have all powers necessary to carry out its purpose, including the power to:
- Acquire, hold, and dispose of property
- Enter into contracts
- Sue and be sued
- Borrow money
- Execute documents

---

## ARTICLE 4: PROFITS, LOSSES, AND DISTRIBUTIONS

### 4.1 Allocation of Profits and Losses
Profits and losses shall be allocated to Members in proportion to their ownership percentages, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### 4.2 Distributions
Distributions shall be made to Members in proportion to their ownership percentages at such times as the Members determine.

### 4.3 Withholding
The Company shall withhold from distributions any amounts required by law.

---

## ARTICLE 5: MANAGEMENT

### 5.1 Management Structure
[CHOOSE ONE:]

**Option A - Member-Managed:**
The Company shall be managed by all Members. Each Member has equal management authority. Major decisions require majority consent.

**Option B - Manager-Managed:**
The Company shall be managed by a Manager(s) elected by Members. The Manager has full authority to bind the Company in ordinary business matters.

### 5.2 Management Authority
The Manager(s) shall have authority to:
- Open bank accounts
- Enter into contracts up to $[LIMIT]
- Hire employees
- Make operational decisions

### 5.3 Major Decisions
The following actions require unanimous Member approval:
- Merging or consolidating with another entity
- Dissolving the Company
- Selling substantially all Company assets
- Admitting new Members
- Amending this Agreement

---

## ARTICLE 6: MEETINGS AND VOTING

### 6.1 Meetings
Members may hold meetings in person, by phone, or by video conference. Meetings shall be held at least annually.

### 6.2 Voting
Each Member shall be entitled to one vote per ownership percentage point. Major decisions require [MAJORITY/UNANIMOUS] consent.

### 6.3 Consent Without Meeting
Members may take actions by written consent signed by all Members.

---

## ARTICLE 7: BOOKS AND RECORDS

### 7.1 Company Books
The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books and records at its principal office.

### 7.2 Access
Each Member has the right to inspect Company books and records during reasonable business hours.

### 7.3 Bank Accounts
All Company funds shall be deposited in accounts at [BANK NAME]. All withdrawals require [NUMBER] signature(s).

---

## ARTICLE 8: TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP

### 8.1 Restrictions
No Member may transfer, assign, or encumber their Membership interest without written consent of all other Members.

### 8.2 Right of First Refusal
If a Member proposes to sell their interest, the other Members have the right of first refusal to purchase it on the same terms.

### 8.3 Permitted Transfers
Members may transfer to immediate family members or affiliates with prior written notice to other Members.

---

## ARTICLE 9: WITHDRAWAL AND DISSOLUTION

### 9.1 Withdrawal
A Member may withdraw from the Company with [90] days written notice. Upon withdrawal, the Company shall distribute the Member's interest within [180] days.

### 9.2 Dissolution
The Company shall dissolve upon:
- Unanimous vote of Members
- Expiration of term (if applicable)
- Judicial order
- Inability to conduct business

### 9.3 Wind-Up
Upon dissolution, Members shall:
- Pay all Company debts
- Distribute remaining assets to Members
- File final tax returns

---

## ARTICLE 10: INDEMNIFICATION

### 10.1 Indemnification
The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless Members and Managers from any claims arising from their good-faith actions on behalf of the Company.

---

## ARTICLE 11: GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 11.1 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of [STATE].

### 11.2 Amendment
This Agreement may be amended by written consent of all Members.

### 11.3 Severability
If any provision is invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in effect.

### 11.4 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement among Members.
