# Copyright Protection Guide

> Everything you need to know about protecting your creative works through copyright registration.

---

## Understanding Copyright Basics

### What Copyright Protects

**Automatically Protected (Upon Creation):**
- Literary works (books, articles, software code)
- Musical compositions
- Dramatic works (plays, movies)
- Pictorial, graphic, and sculptural works
- Motion pictures and audiovisual works
- Sound recordings
- Architectural works

**NOT Protected by Copyright:**
- Ideas, procedures, processes, systems
- Titles, names, short phrases
- Facts and data
- Government works (in most cases)

---

## Why Registration Matters

### Benefits of Federal Registration

| Benefit | Registered | Unregistered |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| File lawsuit in federal court | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Statutory damages | ✅ Up to $150,000 | ❌ Actual damages only |
| Attorney's fees | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Prima facie evidence | ✅ Yes | ❌ Must prove from scratch |
| Public record | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |

**Important:** While copyright exists automatically upon creation, federal registration is required to enforce your rights through litigation.

## Step 2: Determine Authorship

### Single Author
- [ ] Identify the creator's full legal name
- [ ] Document the creation date
- [ ] Note if created as work for hire

### Multiple Authors
- [ ] Identify all contributing authors
- [ ] Determine if contributions are independently copyrightable
- [ ] Document work made for hire status
- [ ] Decide on joint authorship vs. collective work

### Work Made for Hire
A work is "work made for hire" if:
1. Created by an employee within scope of employment, OR
2. Specially commissioned for certain uses with written agreement

---

## Step 3: Publication Status

### Unpublished Works
- [ ] No registration required for copyright to exist
- [ ] Can register as a collection for efficiency
- [ ] No deposit requirement for literary works

### Published Works
- [ ] "Published" means distribution to the public
- [ ] Requires deposit copies with registration
- [ ] Must identify publication date

---

## Step 4: The Registration Process

### Step 4.1: Create an Account

**Go to:** [copyright.gov](https://copyright.gov)

**Steps:**
1. [ ] Create a new account
2. [ ] Verify your email address
3. [ ] Set up two-factor authentication (recommended)

### Step 4.2: Complete the Application

**For Single Work (Form SE):**
1. [ ] Select "Register a Single Work"
2. [ ] Choose the appropriate form
3. [ ] Enter work title (exact title or brief description)
4. [ ] Provide author information
5. [ ] Indicate year of creation
6. [ ] Describe "nature of authorship"
7. [ ] Mark publication status

### Step 4.3: Upload Deposit

**Deposit Requirements by Work Type:**

| Work Type | Deposit Requirement |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Text (unpublished) | One complete copy |
| Text (published) | Two copies of best edition |
| Music/Sound | One complete recording |
| Visual Art | Photo of work or actual copy |
| Software | Source code + object code |

### Step 4.4: Pay the Filing Fee

| Registration Type | Fee |
|------------------|-----|
| Single work (online) | $65 |
| Single work (paper) | $100 |
| Group registration (unpublished) | $85 |
| Group registration (published) | $65/work |

### Step 4.5: Submit and Wait

**Current Processing Times:**
- Online registration: 3-6 months
- Paper registration: 6-10 months

---

## Step 5: Post-Registration

### Certificate of Registration

**Upon approval, you will receive:**
- [ ] Copyright registration number
- [ ] Certificate of registration
- [ ] Public record of your claim

**Keep these records:**
- [ ] Copy of the certificate
- [ ] Correspondence with Copyright Office
- [ ] Proof of filing date

### Infringement Protection

**To sue for infringement, you must:**
1. Registration is complete (not just filed)
2. Original work or derivative work
3. Valid copyright in the work
4. Infringing copying of original elements

## Special Registration Options

### Group Registration for Unpublished Works (GRUW)
- [ ] Register up to 10 unpublished works
- [ ] Single fee: $85
- [ ] Works must be by same author
- [ ] Good for preserving creative backlog

### Series Registration
- [ ] Single application for ongoing series
- [ ] Must have common title or theme
- [ ] Best for episodic content

---

## International Copyright

### Berne Convention Countries

If you register in a Berne Convention country, your copyright is recognized in all 180+ member countries.

**Key Points:**
- [ ] US registration required for US-based work
- [ ] International registration via WIPO
- [ ] Consider local registration for key markets

---

## Copyright Myths vs. Reality

| Myth | Reality |
|------|--------|
| "I don't need to register, I have copyright automatically" | True, but registration required to sue |
| "Putting a © symbol protects me" | Notice helps, registration enforces |
| "Fair use means I can use anything for education" | Fair use is a defense, not a right |
| "If I can't find the author, I can use it" | Orphan works still require licensing |
