# Quick Reference Guide

> One-page summary of key IP protection actions and timelines.

---

## THE 4-SECOND SUMMARY

| Protection Type | Action | Timeline | Cost |
|-----------------|--------|----------|------|
| **Trademark** | File USPTO application | 8-12 months | $250-350 |
| **Copyright** | Register with Copyright Office | 3-6 months | $65 |
| **LLC** | File with Secretary of State | 1-2 weeks | $50-500 |
| **Corporation** | File Certificate of Incorporation | 1-2 weeks | $89+ |

---

## TRADEMARK: 5 STEPS

1. Search USPTO TESS (tess2.uspto.gov)
2. Check state databases
3. Identify your Nice Classification
4. File TEAS Plus ($250/class) or Standard ($350)
5. Respond to any Office Action

**Renew:** Every 10 years (Section 9)

---

## COPYRIGHT: 4 STEPS

1. Identify all copyrightable works
2. Determine author and creation date
3. Submit application at copyright.gov ($65)
4. Save certificate of registration

**Protect:** Automatic upon creation; registration required to sue

---

## LLC FORMATION: 6 STEPS

1. Choose unique name (search state DB)
2. Appoint registered agent
3. File Articles of Organization
4. Create Operating Agreement
5. Get EIN from IRS (free)
6. Open business bank account

**Renew:** Annual reports (varies by state)

---

## CORPORATION FORMATION: 9 STEPS

1. Choose Delaware (recommended for VC)
2. Name search
3. File Certificate of Incorporation ($89)
4. Create bylaws
5. Appoint directors
6. Issue founder stock
7. Hold first board meeting
8. Get EIN
9. Open business account

**Renew:** Annual reports + annual meetings

---

## IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE

```
WEEK 1:  Trademark search + filing
WEEK 2:  LLC/Corp formation
WEEK 3:  Copyright registration
WEEK 4:  Compliance setup + monitoring
```

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ Waiting to file until brand is "established"
❌ Not searching before filing
❌ Wrong Nice Classification
❌ Mixing personal and business finances
❌ Skipping Operating Agreement/Bylaws
❌ Forgetting annual compliance
❌ Not registering copyrights you need to enforce
