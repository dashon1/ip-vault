# Case Study: How Alex Protected His Consulting Business with an LLC

## The Situation

Alex Morrison had been running his marketing consulting business as a sole proprietor for 2 years. He was making $85,000 annually, had 12 regular clients, and leased a small home office. When a client disputed a $4,500 invoice and threatened to sue, Alex realized his personal assets—his car, savings account, even his house—were all on the line.

**What he learned the hard way:** Without an LLC, there was no legal separation between "Alex" and "Alex's business." A lawsuit against his business was a lawsuit against him personally.

He quickly filed his LLC formation documents and was relieved when the client backed down. But he realized he'd waited too long.

**He could have had full liability protection from day one for $150.**

## The Solution: His Complete LLC Formation in 2 Weeks

Alex documented his entire LLC formation process so you can replicate it:

### Day 1: State Selection & Name Search

**Choosing Delaware (Why It Matters)**
- Researched that Delaware has:
  - Court of Chancery (specialized business court)
  - Privacy protections (officers/directors not public record)
  - Business-friendly laws
  - Strong precedent in corporate law
- Decision: File in Delaware despite living in Colorado

**Name Search (1 hour)**
- Went to Delaware Division of Corporations website
- Searched: "Morrison Marketing"
- Found: "MORRISON MARKETING LLC" already exists
- Searched variations: "Alex Morrison Consulting," "Morrison Digital," "Morrison Strategy Group"
- Found: "MORRISON STRATEGY GROUP LLC" — available
- Decision: Use "Morrison Strategy Group LLC"

**Name Reservation (Optional but Recommended)**
- Reserved name for 120 days: $75
- Bought time to set up everything else properly

### Day 2-3: Registered Agent Selection

**Understanding Registered Agents**
- Researched that Delaware requires a registered agent with physical address in Delaware
- Options:
  - Corporation Service Company (CSC): $299/year
  - LegalZoom Registered Agent: $299/year
  - Harvard Business Services: $199/year
  - CT Corporation: $299/year
- Decision: Harvard Business Services ($199/year) for balance of cost and reputation

**What the Agent Does**
- Receives legal documents on behalf of the LLC
- Forwards lawsuit papers and official state correspondence
- Must have physical address (not PO Box) in Delaware
- Must be available during business hours

### Day 4-5: Formation Filing

**Certificate of Formation Preparation**
Delaware requires:
- LLC name (exact spelling)
- Registered agent name and address
- Registered agent consent (signature)
- Principal place of business (can be out of state)
- Organizer information (name, address)
- Duration (perpetual recommended)

**Filing Methods**

| Online | Fastest | $90 + $50 rush | Same day |
| Mail | Slowest | $90 | 5-7 business days |
| Expedited Online | Fastest | $90 + $100 rush | 1-2 hours |

**Total Cost: $140** (Delaware filing) + $199 (registered agent) = **$339**

### Day 6-7: Operating Agreement Creation

**Why Operating Agreement Matters**
- Required by Delaware law for multi-member LLCs
- Crucial for single-member LLCs (establishes separation)
- Defines ownership percentages
- Outlines profit distribution
- Establishes management structure
- Prevents disputes among members

**Alex's Operating Agreement Key Points**

For his single-member LLC, he included:
1. **Member:** Alex Morrison (100% ownership)
2. **Capital Contribution:** $1,000 (initial)
3. **Distributions:** 100% to Alex
4. **Management:** Member-managed
5. **Banking:** Requires Alex's signature
6. **Books:** Maintained at principal place of business
7. **Dissolution:** Alex can dissolve with 30 days notice
8. **Liability:** Member not personally liable for LLC debts

**Time Investment:** 4 hours to draft, 30 minutes to sign and date

### Day 8-9: EIN Application

**IRS EIN (Employer Identification Number)**
- Needed for:
  - Opening business bank account
  - Hiring employees (future)
  - Filing business tax returns
  - Some client contracts require it

**Application Process**
- Went to IRS.gov/ein
- Applied online (instant approval for most)
- EIN: 83-XXXXXXX
- Downloaded confirmation letter (PDF)
- Saved to business folder

**Time:** 15 minutes
**Cost:** $0 (free from IRS)

### Day 10-12: Business Bank Account

**Why Separate Accounts Matter**
- Legal requirement for LLC liability protection
- Mix personal and business = "piercing the corporate veil"
- Required by most accounting software
- Makes tax time much easier

**Bank Selection Process**
Compared 5 banks:
| Bank | Monthly Fee | Minimum | Features |
|------|-------------|---------|----------|
| Chase | $15 | $1,500 | Good app, branches |
| Bank of America | $12 | $1,500 | Rewards available |
| Mercury | $0 | $0 | Online-only, free |
| Bluevine | $0 | $500 | High-yield option |
| Novo | $0 | $0 | Good integrations |

**Decision: Mercury** (online banking, free, good for consultants)
- Applied online with:
  - LLC Certificate of Formation (PDF)
  - EIN letter (PDF)
  - Operating Agreement (PDF)
  - Alex's driver's license
- Account approved: Day 12
- Routing/Account numbers received: Day 12

### Day 13-14: Business Infrastructure

**Accounting Setup**
- Created Mercury account for business income
- Set up Wave (free) for invoicing and expense tracking
- Created separate categories:
  - Consulting Revenue
  - Software Subscriptions
  - Office Expenses
  - Professional Services

**Client Communication Update**
- Created new email: alex@morrisonstrategies.com
- Set up Google Workspace ($6/month)
- Updated email signature on all communications

**Contract Update**
- Added LLC name to all new contracts
- Changed payment terms to "Payable to Morrison Strategy Group LLC"
- Reviewed existing contracts for liability provisions

## Alex's Key Takeaways

### What He Wishes He'd Known Sooner:

1. **The $150 vs. $414 decision**
   - "I could have filed myself for $140 plus agent fees. The DIY route is fine if you're organized."

2. **Delaware isn't always necessary**
   - "If you're only operating in one state, filing there is often cheaper. I wanted the legal protections Delaware offers consulting businesses."

3. **Operating Agreement is critical**
   - "Even as a single member, having this document proves your LLC is real. It's not optional."

4. **Keep business and personal separate**
   - "This is the #1 rule. Separate accounts, separate credit cards, separate everything."

5. **Annual report deadlines**
   - "Delaware requires an annual report ($300). I set a calendar reminder 60 days before due."
