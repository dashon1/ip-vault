# Trademark Registration Checklist

> A comprehensive step-by-step guide for registering your trademark with the USPTO.

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Filing Research

### Step 1.1: Conduct a USPTO TESS Search

Before filing, you must search the USPTO's Trademark Electronic Search System (TESS).

**Search Strategy:**
- [ ] Search for exact word matches
- [ ] Search for phonetic equivalents
- [ ] Search for similar designs/symbols
- [ ] Check in your specific class (Nice Classification)
- [ ] Review both "Live" and "Dead" applications

**Where to Search:** [tess2.uspto.gov](https://tess2.uspto.gov)

**Time Required:** 2-4 hours for comprehensive search

---

### Step 1.2: State-Level Search

Many states maintain their own trademark databases that may conflict with your proposed mark.

**Search Checklist:**
- [ ] Secretary of State database in your primary business state
- [ ] Key states where you plan to operate
- [ ] States where competitors are registered

---

### Step 1.3: Common Law Search

Search for businesses using your mark even if not registered.

**Search Areas:**
- [ ] Business name databases in your state
- [ ] Domain name registrars (who.is)
- [ ] Social media platforms (Instagram, Twitter, Facebook)
- [ ] Google search for exact matches and variations

---

## Phase 2: Determine Your Filing Basis

### Understanding Filing Bases

| Filing Basis | Description | When to Use |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Use in Commerce** (Section 1(a)) | Currently using the mark in business | You are actively using the mark |
| **Intent to Use** (Section 1(b)) | Planning to use but not yet | Development stage, want priority date |

**Recommendation:** Use "Intent to Use" if you're close to launching - it secures your priority date while you prepare to use the mark.

---

## Phase 3: Identify Your Nice Classification

Trademarks are filed under specific product/service categories called Nice Classifications. There are 45 classes.

### Common Classifications

| Class | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Class 35 | Advertising; Business Management |
| Class 41 | Education; Entertainment |
| Class 42 | Science & Technology Services |
| Class 9 | Computer Hardware & Software |
| Class 25 | Clothing & Apparel |

**Action Required:**
- [ ] Identify all classes relevant to your business
- [ ] Consider future expansion classes
- [ ] Document your classification decision

---

## Phase 4: Prepare Your Application

### Step 4.1: Gather Required Information

**For Individual/Founder:**
- [ ] Full legal name
- [ ] Mailing address
- [ ] Email address

**For Business Entity:**
- [ ] Legal business name (exactly as registered)
- [ ] State of incorporation/formation
- [ ] Entity type (LLC, Corporation, etc.)
- [ ] Authorized signatory information

### Step 4.2: Prepare Specimen Samples

A specimen shows how you actually use the mark in commerce.

**Acceptable Specimens:**
- [ ] Product labels with the mark
- [ ] Website screenshot showing the mark
- [ ] Business cards with the logo
- [ ] Packaging with the mark

**Requirements:**
- Must show the mark as used in commerce
- Must be a real example, not mockup
- Must match exactly with your filing

---

## Phase 5: File Your Application

### Filing Options

| Option | Cost (per class) | Features |
|--------|-----------------|----------|
| **TEAS Plus** | $250 | Must select from menu; lower cost |
| **TEAS Standard** | $350 | More flexibility in description |

**Filing Steps:**
1. [ ] Create USPTO.gov account
2. [ ] Select filing basis (Use or Intent to Use)
3. [ ] Enter mark information (text or image)
4. [ ] Select Nice Classification(s)
5. [ ] Enter goods/services description
6. [ ] Upload specimen samples
7. [ ] Review and pay filing fee

---

## Phase 6: Post-Filing Timeline

### What Happens After Filing

| Stage | Timeframe | What to Do |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Initial Review** | 1-2 weeks | Check for receipt confirmation |
| **Examination** | 3-4 months | Wait for USPTO examining attorney |
| **Office Action** | If needed | Respond within 6 months |
| **Publication** | 30 days | Public notice period |
| **Registration** | 8-12 months total | Receive certificate |

**Critical Deadlines:**
- [ ] Respond to Office Actions within 6 months
- [ ] File Statement of Use before deadline (Intent to Use)
- [ ] Maintain registration every 10 years

---

## Phase 7: Maintain Your Registration

### Post-Registration Requirements

| Requirement | When | Purpose |
|-------------|------|---------|
| **Section 8 Declaration** | Years 5-6 | Continue using the mark |
| **Section 9 Renewal** | Every 10 years | Extend protection |
| **Section 15 Declaration** | Years 5-6 | Declare incontestability |

## Checklist Summary

### Pre-Filing
- [ ] USPTO TESS search completed
- [ ] State database search completed
- [ ] Common law search completed
- [ ] Classification(s) identified
- [ ] Specimen samples prepared

### Filing
- [ ] TEAS Plus or Standard application filed
- [ ] Filing fee paid
- [ ] Receipt confirmation saved

### Post-Filing
- [ ] Monitor application status
- [ ] Respond to any Office Actions
- [ ] File Statement of Use (if Intent to Use)
- [ ] Schedule maintenance deadlines

---

## Pro Tips

1. **File Early:** Priority goes to the first to file, not first to use
2. **Consider Design Mark:** If you have a logo, file both word and design marks
3. **Watch Similar Marks:** Set up monitoring for confusingly similar marks
4. **Use an Attorney:** Complex cases benefit from professional guidance
