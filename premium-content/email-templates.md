**Mark Details:**
- **Registration Number:** [INSERT]
- **Mark:** [INSERT]
- **Renewal Deadline:** [DATE]

**What to Do:**
1. Verify you're still using the mark in commerce
2. Gather specimen showing current use
3. File Section 8 Declaration before deadline
