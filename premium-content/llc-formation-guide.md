# LLC Formation Guide

> A comprehensive walkthrough for forming and maintaining a Limited Liability Company.

---

## Why Form an LLC?

### Benefits of LLC Structure

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Limited Liability** | Personal assets protected from business debts |
| **Pass-Through Taxation** | No double taxation; profits taxed once |
| **Flexibility** | Can choose how you're taxed (LLC, S-Corp, C-Corp) |
| **Less Formal** | Fewer meetings and record-keeping than corporations |
| **Credibility** | Professional structure for clients and partners |

---

## Step 1: Name Your LLC

### Naming Requirements

**Your LLC name MUST:**
- [ ] Include "Limited Liability Company" or abbreviation (LLC)
- [ ] Be distinguishable from existing businesses
- [ ] Not be reserved by another entity
- [ ] Not contain restricted words (bank, insurance, etc.)

### Name Search Checklist
- [ ] Search your state's LLC database
- [ ] Check USPTO for trademark conflicts
- [ ] Verify domain name availability
- [ ] Check social media handles

## Step 2: Choose Your State

### Factors to Consider

| Factor | Implication |
|--------|-------------|
| **Filing Fee** | Ranges from $50 to $500 |
| **Annual Fees** | Recurring costs vary significantly |
| **Tax Rates** | State income taxes differ |
| **Court System** | Some states have more business-friendly courts |
| **Your Location** | Must have registered agent in state |

**Recommendation:** Form in your home state unless you have specific reasons for another state (Nevada, Wyoming, Delaware for privacy/tax reasons).

---

## Step 3: Designate a Registered Agent

### What is a Registered Agent?

A registered agent is a person or company authorized to receive legal documents on behalf of your LLC.

**Requirements:**
- [ ] Must have physical address in formation state
- [ ] Must be available during business hours
- [ ] Can be yourself, another person, or a service

### Registered Agent Services
- [ ] Many operate nationwide
- [ ] Annual fees typically $100-$300
- [ ] Provides privacy and convenience

---

## Step 4: File Articles of Organization

### What to Include

**Required Information:**
- [ ] LLC name
- [ ] Registered agent name and address
- [ ] Principal place of business address
- [ ] Duration (perpetual or specific end date)
- [ ] Signature of authorized person

**Optional (but recommended):**
- [ ] Management structure (member vs manager managed)
- [ ] Purpose of the LLC
- [ ] Names of initial members

### Filing Checklist
1. [ ] Draft Articles of Organization
2. [ ] Pay state filing fee (varies by state)
3. [ ] Submit to Secretary of State
4. [ ] Receive certified copy/filer number

---

## Step 5: Create an Operating Agreement

### Why You Need One

An Operating Agreement is an internal document that outlines how your LLC will operate.

**Key Provisions:**

| Section | What to Include |
|---------|----------------|
| **Ownership** | Members and their percentages |
| **Management** | Who runs the LLC day-to-day |
| **Voting** | How decisions are made |
| **Profits/Losses** | How money is distributed |
| **Meetings** | How often and how held |
| **Transfers** | What happens when a member leaves |

### Operating Agreement Checklist
- [ ] List all members with contact info
- [ ] Define profit/loss allocation
- [ ] Outline voting rights
- [ ] Specify management structure
- [ ] Include buyout provisions
- [ ] Add dispute resolution clause

---

## Step 6: Get an EIN

### Employer Identification Number

An EIN is a federal tax ID number for your LLC.

**How to Get:**
1. [ ] Go to IRS.gov
2. [ ] Apply online (free, instant approval)
3. [ ] Receive EIN immediately

**When You Need EIN:**
- [ ] Hiring employees
- [ ] Opening business bank account
- [ ] Filing tax returns
- [ ] Most business licenses

---

## Step 7: Open a Business Bank Account

### Why This Matters

Separating personal and business finances is essential for maintaining LLC liability protection.

**Required Documents:**
- [ ] LLC formation documents
- [ ] EIN confirmation letter
- [ ] Operating Agreement
- [ ] Government ID

### Bank Account Checklist
- [ ] Research business-friendly banks
- [ ] Compare fees and services
- [ ] Schedule appointment
- [ ] Bring all documents
- [ ] Set up online banking

## Step 9: LLC Tax Considerations

### Default: Pass-Through Taxation

**The LLC itself does not pay income tax.** Profits and losses "pass through" to members who report on personal tax returns.

**Tax Form:** Schedule C attached to Form 1040

### Alternative: S-Corp Election

**When to Consider:**
- LLC profits exceeding $80,000/year
- Want to reduce self-employment tax
- Reinvesting profits into business

**Requirements:**
- [ ] File Form 2553 with IRS
- [ ] All members must be US citizens/residents
- [ ] No more than 100 shareholders

## LLC vs. Other Structures

### LLC vs. Sole Proprietorship

| Factor | Sole Prop | LLC |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| Liability | Unlimited | Limited |
| Taxes | Pass-through | Pass-through |
| Paperwork | Minimal | Moderate |
| Cost to Form | Free | $50-$500 |

### LLC vs. Corporation

| Factor | LLC | C-Corp |
|--------|-----|--------|
| Ownership | Members | Shareholders |
| Management | Flexible | Formal |
| Taxes | Pass-through | Double |
| Investor Ready | Limited | Yes |
