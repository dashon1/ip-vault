# Case Study: How Maya Secured Her Jewelry Brand for $250

## The Situation

Maya Chen had been selling handcrafted jewelry on Etsy for 18 months under the name "Luna & Stone." Her pieces were gaining popularity, and she was averaging $3,200 in monthly sales. Then she received a cease-and-desist letter from a larger company claiming her brand name infringed on their trademark.

**What she learned the hard way:** They had filed their trademark 6 months before she started using "Luna & Stone."

Maya had to rebrand entirely—new logo, new packaging, new social media accounts, and had to tell 1,400 existing customers about the change. Total cost: **$8,400** in rebranding expenses and lost sales.

**She could have avoided all of this for $250.**

---

## The Solution: Her Step-by-Step Blueprint

After rebuilding under "Celestial Gems," Maya followed this exact process to secure her trademark:

### Week 1: Search & Strategy

**Day 1-2: USPTO TESS Search**
- Went to [tess2.uspto.gov](https://tess2.uspto.gov)
- Searched: "Luna" AND "Stone" AND "Celestial" AND "Gems"
- Checked in Classes 14 (jewelry), 26 (hair ornaments), 40 (custom manufacturing)
- Found 3 similar marks in Class 14 that needed analysis
- Downloaded all conflicting application details

**Day 3-4: State Database Search**
- Searched California Secretary of State business database
- Searched New York, Texas, Florida (major markets)
- Found 2 potential conflicts in California—marked for legal review

**Day 5: Social Media & Domain Check**
- Checked Instagram, Facebook, TikTok for @CelestialGems
- Checked Namechk for domain availability
- All clear—she was able to secure @CelestialGems across all platforms

**Day 6-7: Classification Decision**
- Identified her products: handcrafted jewelry, gemstone pieces, custom pieces
- Chose Nice Classification 14 (precious metals, jewelry)
- Also registered Class 40 (custom manufacturing of jewelry)
- Decision: TEAS Plus application ($250 per class) = $500 total

### Week 2: Application Preparation

**Day 8-9: Specimen Collection**
- Took professional photos of jewelry on model
- Captured lifestyle shots of her workspace
- Gathered product tags showing brand name
- Ensured images clearly displayed "Celestial Gems" branding

**Day 10: Specimen Rejection Prevention**
- Created dedicated product photography with clean backgrounds
- Added "Celestial Gems" hang tags to all pieces
- Uploaded images to Etsy with watermark showing brand
- Made sure specimens showed mark "in use in commerce"

**Day 11: Drawing/Image Preparation**
- Created black and white version of logo for USPTO
- Ensured image met 300 DPI requirement
- Saved as JPG format for TEAS Plus

**Day 12-14: Application Draft**
- Completed TEAS Plus online application
- Entered all owner information (her name, address)
- Listed all goods and services in proper description format
- Uploaded specimens showing use in commerce
- Attached black and white drawing of mark

### Week 3: Filing & Follow-Up

**Day 15: Application Submission**
- Submitted TEAS Plus application
- Payment: $500 (2 classes × $250)
- Received confirmation number: 97XXXXX2024
- Set calendar reminder for 3-month review window

**Day 16-20: Waiting Period**
- Received filing receipt via email (Day 17)
- USPTO assigned examining attorney (Day 19)
- Created tracking spreadsheet with all application numbers

**Day 21-25: Office Action Response (If Needed)**
- Maya's application required a minor amendment to description
- Filed response within 2 weeks using EFS-Web
- Cost: $0 (amendments are free)

### Months 2-4: Approval Process

**Month 2:**
- Checked TESS weekly for application status
- Status changed to "Published for Opposition"
- Set 30-day reminder to monitor for any challenges

**Month 3:**
- No opposition filed
- Received Notice of Allowance
- Prepared and filed Statement of Use showing specimens in commerce
- USPTO accepted specimens

**Month 4:**
- Received registration certificate
- Downloaded and saved PDF to cloud storage
- Added to business insurance documentation
- Updated product packaging with ® symbol

## Maya's Key Takeaways

### What She Did Right the Second Time:

1. **Searched BEFORE using the name**
   - "Check TESS before you pick your brand. It's free and takes 20 minutes."

2. **Filed TEAS Plus for multiple classes**
   - "I should have protected jewelry AND custom manufacturing from day one."

3. **Kept specimens ready**
   - "Professional product photos paid for themselves when I had them ready for the USPTO."

4. **Set calendar reminders**
   - "I tracked every deadline. Missing a response deadline means starting over."

5. **Used the registration properly**
   - Added ® symbol to all materials
   - Monitored for infringers quarterly
   - Renewed before the 5-6 year deadline

## The Complete Checklist (Your Copy)

Use this exact checklist to replicate Maya's success:

### Pre-Filing (Week 1)
- [ ] Search USPTO TESS database (tess2.uspto.gov)
- [ ] Search your state business database
- [ ] Search social media platforms for your name
- [ ] Check domain registrar for availability
- [ ] Identify your Nice Classification(s)
- [ ] Document all search results with screenshots
- [ ] Assess any potential conflicts with legal review

### Application Prep (Week 2)
- [ ] Create professional specimens showing mark in use
- [ ] Prepare black and white drawing of logo (300 DPI)
- [ ] Draft goods/services description in proper format
- [ ] Gather owner information (full legal name, address)
- [ ] Decide between TEAS Plus ($250) or Standard ($350)
- [ ] Prepare credit card for online payment

### Filing (Week 3)
- [ ] Complete TEAS Plus application online
- [ ] Upload specimens and drawings
- [ ] Review all information for accuracy
- [ ] Submit and save confirmation number
- [ ] Set calendar reminders for:
  - 3-month review period
  - 30-day opposition period (after publication)
  - 6-month response deadline (if Office Action)
  - 5-year Section 8 deadline

### Post-Registration
- [ ] Download and save registration certificate (PDF)
- [ ] Update all branded materials with ® symbol
- [ ] Add trademark to business insurance file
- [ ] Set annual reminder to search for infringers
- [ ] Plan for 10-year renewal
- [ ] Document all enforcement actions taken
