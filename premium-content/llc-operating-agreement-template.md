# ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS

**1.1 "Company"** means [LLC NAME] LLC, the limited liability company formed hereby.

**1.2 "Operating Agreement"** means this Agreement, as amended from time to time.

**1.3 "Member"** means any person or entity that has been admitted as a member of the Company and has not withdrawn or been expelled.

**1.4 "Capital Account"** means the individual account maintained for each Member as described in Section 3.4.

**1.5 "Distributable Cash"** means all cash of the Company not reserved for working capital or other proper purposes.

**1.6 "Majority Vote"** means more than 50% of the voting interests of the Members.

**1.7 "Unanimous Consent"** means the consent of all Members.

(a) Credited with: (i) the Member's initial capital contribution, (ii) additional capital contributions, and (iii) the Member's allocable share of Company profits;

# ARTICLE VI: BOOKS AND RECORDS

**6.1 Company Books**
The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books and records at its principal place of business, including:
(a) A current list of Members with their addresses;
(b) Copies of the Articles of Organization and this Agreement;
(c) Financial statements for the past [3] years;
(d) Minutes of all Member meetings;
(e) All contracts to which the Company is a party;
(f) All federal, state, and local tax returns for the past [4] years.

**6.2 Member Inspection Rights**
Each Member shall have the right to inspect Company books and records during reasonable business hours upon [48 hours/5 days] advance written notice.

**6.3 Financial Reports**
The Manager/Members shall provide to each Member:
(a) Quarterly financial statements within 30 days of quarter end;
(b) Annual financial statements within 60 days of fiscal year end;
(c) Schedule K-1 (Partner/Member Share of Income) within 90 days of fiscal year end.

**6.4 Bank Accounts**
All Company funds shall be deposited in accounts at [BANK NAME]. Withdrawals require [one/two] signature(s) from authorized signatories: [List authorized signatories].

Each Member shall be entitled to vote in proportion to their ownership percentage as set forth in Section 3.3.

**7.4 Action by Written Consent**

**7.5 Quorum**

**7.6 Meetings by Communication Equipment**
Members may participate in any meeting by telephone, video conference, or similar communication equipment that allows all participants to hear each other.

# ARTICLE IX: TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST

**9.1 Restrictions on Transfer**
No Member may sell, assign, transfer, pledge, hypothecate, or otherwise dispose of their Membership interest without:

(a) Written consent of [all/percentage] other Members; and
(b) Compliance with applicable securities laws.

**9.2 Right of First Refusal**
If a Member proposes to sell their Membership interest, the other Members shall have the right of first refusal to purchase such interest on the same terms and conditions offered by the proposed purchaser.

**9.3 Right of First Refusal Procedure**
The selling Member shall give written notice to the Company and other Members specifying:
(a) The identity of the proposed purchaser;
(b) The purchase price;
(c) All other terms and conditions of the proposed sale.

The other Members shall have [15/30] days to exercise their right of first refusal on the same terms.

**9.4 Permitted Transfers**
Notwithstanding Section 9.1, a Member may transfer their Membership interest to:
(a) Immediate family members (spouse, children, parents, siblings);
(b) A trust for the benefit of the Member or their immediate family;
(c) Another existing Member;
without requiring consent of other Members, provided the transferring Member gives [10/15/30] days prior written notice.

**9.5 Effect of Transfer**
Any transferee shall be admitted as a substitute Member only upon executing this Agreement or an amendment adding their name as a Member.

# ARTICLE X: WITHDRAWAL AND DISSOLUTION

**10.1 Voluntary Withdrawal**
A Member may withdraw from the Company by giving [90/180] days written notice to all other Members. Upon withdrawal, the Company shall purchase the withdrawing Member's interest as provided in Section 10.3.

**10.2 Involuntary Withdrawal**
A Member shall be deemed to have withdrawn upon:
(a) Death or disability;
(b) Bankruptcy or insolvency;
(c) Expulsion pursuant to Section 10.2.1;
(d) Voluntary dissolution;
(e) Assignment for benefit of creditors.

**10.2.1 Expulsion for Cause**
A Member may be expelled by [unanimous vote of other Members/percentage] if:
(a) The Member materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 30 days of notice;
(b) The Member engages in conduct that adversely affects the Company;
(c) The Member is convicted of a felony or crime involving moral turpitude.

**10.3 Buyout of Withdrawn Member**
Upon any withdrawal, the remaining Members shall purchase the withdrawing Member's interest at fair market value as determined by:
(a) Mutual agreement within 60 days; or
(b) If no agreement, by an independent appraiser selected by [mutual agreement/court].

Payment shall be made in [lump sum/equal installments over X years] with [interest rate/zero interest].

**10.4 Dissolution Events**
The Company shall dissolve upon the first to occur of:
(a) Unanimous vote of Members;
(b) The expiration of a term specified in the Articles of Organization;
(c) Judicial decree of dissolution;
(d) The death, withdrawal, or expulsion of the last Member;
(e) Any event making it unlawful to continue business.

**10.5 Wind-Up Procedures**
Upon dissolution, the Manager/Members shall:
(a) Wind up Company affairs;
(b) Pay or make provision for all Company debts and liabilities;
(c) Distribute remaining assets in the following order:
   (i) To creditors, including Members as creditors;
   (ii) To Members for any undistributed Distributable Cash;
   (iii) To Members in proportion to their positive Capital Account balances;
   (iv) Any remainder to Members in proportion to their ownership percentages.

**10.6 Continuing Authority**
During winding up, the Manager/Members shall have authority to:
(a) Execute documents to transfer title to assets;
(b) Collect outstanding debts;
(c) Institute legal actions in Company name;
(d) Take all other actions necessary for wind-up.

---

# ARTICLE XI: INDEMNIFICATION

**11.1 Indemnification**
The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless each Member and their officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any claims, liabilities, damages, losses, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from their good-faith actions on behalf of the Company.

**11.2 No Indemnification**
No Member shall be indemnified for:
(a) Acts of fraud or willful misconduct;
(b) Breach of this Agreement;
(c) Violations of fiduciary duties.

**11.3 Advancement of Expenses**
The Company may advance expenses to indemnified parties, provided such party agrees to repay if found not entitled to indemnification.

**11.4 Insurance**
The Company may purchase insurance to cover indemnification obligations.

---

# ARTICLE XII: MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Governing Law**
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of [STATE].

**12.2 Amendment**
This Agreement may be amended by written consent of Members holding [majority/unanimous] of ownership interests.

**12.3 Severability**
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**12.4 Entire Agreement**
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement among Members and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

**12.5 Binding Effect**
This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Members and their respective heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns.

**12.6 Notices**
All notices required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by:
(a) Personal delivery;
(b) Certified mail, return receipt requested;
(c) Overnight courier (FedEx, UPS);
(d) Email with read receipt confirmation.

Notices shall be sent to the addresses set forth in Section 3.1.

**12.7 Counterparts**
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one agreement.

**12.8 Further Assurances**
Each Member agrees to execute any documents and take any actions reasonably necessary to carry out the purposes of this Agreement.

**12.9 Attorneys' Fees**
In any action to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

**12.10 Construction**
This Agreement shall be construed without regard to any presumption against the drafter. headings are for convenience only.
