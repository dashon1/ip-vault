Forced to Rebrand From Scratch

Someone else files your business name as a trademark. You get a cease-and-desist letter. Now you have to rename everything—logo, website, social media, products—costing you months of work and thousands in rebrand expenses.

Average loss: $15,000-50,000 in rebranding costs + lost brand equity

Can't Stop Copycats

Someone steals your content, product design, or creative work. You want to sue—but without copyright registration, you can't file in federal court. Your only option is expensive litigation with limited damages.

Without registration: Can only recover actual damages, not statutory penalties

Without an LLC or corporation, your personal savings, home, and car are on the line if someone sues your business. One lawsuit—even a frivolous one—can wipe out everything you've built.

Exposure: Your home, car, savings, and retirement accounts
