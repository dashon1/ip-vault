The Problem

Your Business Is at Risk Without Proper IP Protection

Every day you delay protecting your intellectual property is another day someone could steal what you've worked so hard to build.

Losing Your Brand Name

Without a registered trademark, someone else can file for your business name. You'll be forced to rebrand or face legal battles—or worse, close your doors.

Can't Sue for Infringement

Without copyright registration, you cannot file a lawsuit in federal court. Even if someone steals your content, your hands are tied.

Personal Liability Exposure

Operating without an LLC or corporation means your personal assets—your home, car, savings—are at risk if someone sues your business.

Costly Mistakes

Without a clear path, entrepreneurs spend thousands in legal fees trying to fix problems that proper planning could have prevented.
