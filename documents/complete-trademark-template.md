# SECTION 1: BEFORE YOU FILE

## 1.1 USPTO TESS Search Checklist

**Step 1: Access TESS**
URL: https://tess2.uspto.gov/
Click: "Basic Word Mark Search (New User)"

**Step 2: Your Search Terms**

| Search Field | Enter This | Why |
|-------------|------------|-----|
| Search Term | YOURBRAND | Exact match |
| Field | WORD | Searches word marks |
| Deadjunct? | YES | Include variations |

**Step 3: Additional Searches to Run**

| Search Type | Example | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Sound-alike | YOURBRAND | Phonetic matches |
| Misspellings | YORBRAND, YORUBRAND | Common errors |
| Plural/singular | YOURBRANDS | Variations |
| Combined words | YOUR+BRAND | Split marks |

**Step 4: Analyze Results**

For each conflicting mark found, determine:
- Same/similar goods or services?
- Same/similar customers?
- Similar appearance or sound?
- Likelihood of confusion?

# SECTION 3: SPECIMEN PREPARATION

## 3.1 What Qualifies as Specimen

| Format | Examples | Requirements |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| Product labels | Hang tags, stickers | Shows mark on product |
| Product packaging | Boxes, bags, wrappers | Mark visible to consumer |
| Website screenshot | Homepage, product page | Shows mark in commerce |
| Point-of-sale display | Signs at purchase location | Shows use at sale |
| Advertising materials | Brochures, catalogs | Shows use in marketing |

## 3.2 Specimen Requirements Checklist

- [ ] Shows your exact trademark as registered
- [ ] Shows the mark in use in commerce
- [ ] Is a realistic representation of how you use the mark
- [ ] Is NOT mockup/digital creation only
- [ ] Includes actual product/service or realistic display
- [ ] Image quality: 300 DPI minimum
- [ ] Format: JPG or PNG

## 3.3 Common Specimen Mistakes

❌ Stock photos with your logo added
❌ Mockup images showing products that don't exist
❌ Social media posts with logo overlay
❌ Business cards without actual products shown
❌ Pure text advertisements without product reference

# SECTION 7: ENFORCEMENT TEMPLATES

## 7.1 Cease and Desist Letter

```
[Your Name/Company Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Email]

[Date]

[Infringer's Name/Company]
[Infringer's Address]
[City, State ZIP]

Re: Cease and Desist - Infringement of U.S. Trademark [Registration Number]

Dear [Infringer's Name/General Counsel]:

I/We are the owner of U.S. Trademark Registration No. [Number]
for the mark "[MARK]" ("Our Mark"), registered on [Date] in
International Class [Class Number] for [Goods/Services Description].

We recently became aware that you are using the mark "[INFRINGING MARK]"
which is substantially similar to Our Mark in connection with
[goods/services]. Your use of this mark constitutes trademark
infringement under 15 U.S.C. § 1114.

We demand that you immediately:
1. Cease all use of the mark "[INFRINGING MARK]" or any mark
   confusingly similar to Our Mark;
2. Remove all infringing materials from your website, social media,
   and any other platforms;
3. Destroy all remaining inventory, packaging, and promotional
   materials bearing the infringing mark.

If you fail to comply within fourteen (14) days of this letter,
we will have no choice but to pursue all available legal remedies,
including injunctive relief and damages.

We hope to resolve this matter without litigation.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Name]
[Title]
[Company Name]
```
