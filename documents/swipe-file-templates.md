## 1.4 Cease and Desist Letter

```
[CERTIFIED MAIL - RETURN RECEIPT REQUESTED]

[DATE]

[INFRINGER'S NAME]
[INFRINGER'S ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

Re: Cease and Desist - U.S. Trademark Infringement
Our Mark: [MARK NAME] - Registration No. [NUMBER]

Dear [INFRINGER NAME],

We represent [YOUR NAME/COMPANY NAME], owner of U.S.
Trademark Registration No. [NUMBER] for the mark "[MARK NAME]"
covering [GOODS/SERVICES IN CLASS X].

We recently discovered that you are using "[INFRINGING MARK]"
which is substantially similar to our client's federally
registered trademark in connection with [GOODS/SERVICES].

YOUR USE OF THIS MARK CONSTITUTES TRADEMARK INFRINGEMENT
UNDER 15 U.S.C. § 1114.

DEMAND

We demand that you immediately:

1. CEASE all use of the mark "[INFRINGING MARK]" and any
   confusingly similar marks;

2. REMOVE all materials bearing the infringing mark from
   your website, social media, packaging, and any other
   platforms within [10/14/30] days;

3. CONFIRM compliance in writing to the address below.

FAILURE TO COMPLY:

If you fail to comply with this demand, our client will
pursue all available legal remedies, including:

- Preliminary and permanent injunctive relief
- Actual damages and infringer's profits
- Statutory damages up to $2,000,000 per mark
- Attorney's fees and court costs
- Criminal penalties for willful infringement

We prefer to resolve this matter without litigation.
Please contact us within [10/14/30] days to discuss.

Regards,

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR TITLE]
[COMPANY NAME]
[ADDRESS]
[PHONE]
[EMAIL]

Enclosures:
- USPTO Registration Certificate for [MARK NAME]
- Examples of infringing use
```

```

Hi [Name],

Thank you for reaching out regarding [the claim/notice].

I've reviewed the content in question: [URL or description]

RESPONSE TO CLAIM:

[Choose one:]

OPTION A - Valid claim, removing content:
"I acknowledge your copyright claim. I am removing the
content from [platform]. The material was [brief reason -
e.g., uploaded in error, not aware of copyright]. I have
removed [specific content] as of [date]."

OPTION B - Disputing claim:
"I dispute this copyright claim. [Brief explanation - e.g.,
I am the original creator, content is in public domain,
content is fair use]. I am providing [evidence/documentation]
to support my position."

OPTION C - Requesting more information:
"I would like to review the specific claim. Please provide:
1. The exact original work you claim is infringed
2. Your registration number for the copyrighted work
3. The specific dates of your copyrighted work's creation"

Please advise on next steps.

Best,
```
